## 2. Mechanical conversions (unhighlighted, no wording change)

The fidelity check passes on these, comparing word tokens with 0 unmarked differences.

- **Citations.**
  - `[n]`, `[9, 10]`, `[7], [8]`, `[9] [10]`, `[7,8]` and `[12]–[14]` become `\cite{...}`, rendered by sn-mathphys-num as `[n]` or compressed ranges.

- **Cross-references.**
  - Roman numerals become Arabic through `\ref`: Section II → 2, Section VIII-C → 8.3, Section IX-A → 9.1, Table I–VII → Table 1–7.
  - "Fig. N" is kept as "Fig.~\ref{figN}", including "Fig. 7(a)/(b)".
  - Figure and table caption numbers ("1.", "I.") are now automatic.
- **Escaping.**
  - `_` in identifiers becomes `\_`; `%` becomes `\%`; record shapes `{…}` become `\{…\}`.
  - OT1 text-mode traps (G09): `|` becomes `$\mid$`, `>` becomes `$>$`, and p-values are written as inline math (`$p < 0.001$`, `$p = 0.00024$`).
- **Headings.**
  - IEEE Roman/letter markers and small caps are dropped, as are the trailing colons on the §2.1–2.3 headings.
  - Run-in bold heads are kept as `\textbf{}` (Review method., P1–P6, Test-suite performance., Baseline and repair policy., Iteration budget., Latency and cost., Explainability., Failure modes., Practical meaning of the metrics., Construct./Internal./External./Conclusion.).
  - "Notable Implementation Properties:" is kept as a plain paragraph.
  - The IEEE run-in labels "Abstract—" and "Keywords—" are dropped; the text now sits in `\abstract{}` and `\keywords{}`.
- **List markers.**
  - The Introduction contributions (Heading-3 paragraphs in the .docx) are an `enumerate` with no subheadings (G19/K03).
  - §4.3 "1)–3)" and §5.1 "1)–4)" are `enumerate`.
  - The §5.3 guards and §7 properties are `itemize`.
